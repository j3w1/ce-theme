# Icons

Original outlined icons for Casa Elida: 24 × 24 grid, 1.7px stroke, round caps and joins, no fill. The SVG files are drawn in ink `#111111`; inline them with `stroke="currentColor"` to take the text color.

- Navigation and actions: `arrow` (flip horizontally for “back”), `chevron`, `menu`, `search`, `filter`, `close`, `plus`, `minus`, `check`, `copy`, `download`, `share`, `trash`.
- Commerce: `bag`, `bag-plus` (add to bag), `heart` (favourites; fill it when pressed), `tag` (offers), `gift`.
- Store and service: `user`, `store`, `pin`, `truck`, `clock`, `chat`, `info`, `home`.
- Product facts: `ruler`, `material`, `grid`, `sun`.

Pair an icon with a word or give its button an `aria-label`. Hide decorative icons with `aria-hidden="true"`. Social platforms are named in text, not drawn with their marks.
