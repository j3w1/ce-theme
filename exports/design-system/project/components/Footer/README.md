# Footer

The storefront footer on the subtle surface: brand intro, link groups and the legal row.

## Classes

- `ce-site-footer` > `ce-container` > `ce-footer-grid`: one column on phones, two from 640px, intro plus three groups from 1024px.
- `ce-footer-intro`: the black wordmark (`logo-wordmark-black.png`), a short line and `ce-social-links` (platforms named in text buttons, not drawn with their marks).
- `ce-footer-links`: 36px link rows in secondary text. `ce-footer-group` (`details`) can collapse groups on phones.
- `ce-footer-legal`: copyright and legal links in 12px, above a hairline.
- Often preceded by `ce-feedback` (“¿Encontraste lo que buscabas?”).

## The consumer supplies

The link groups, legal pages (including the Libro de Reclamaciones), social profiles and accepted payment methods when they are confirmed.

## Do and don't

- Do keep link text short and specific.
- Don't add payment or delivery-partner logos without their usage permission.
