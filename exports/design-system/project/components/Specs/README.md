# Specs

Short attribute rows (size or capacity, material) with outlined icons, and the store-availability line.

## Classes

- `ce-specs` (`<dl>`) with `ce-spec` rows: an icon in `dt` with a visually hidden term (“Medida”, “Material”) and the value in `dd`. Icons: `ruler` for size or capacity, `material` for material (Assets → Icons).
- `ce-availability`: 12px semibold secondary text for supplied availability, such as “Disponible en todas las sedes.” or “Disponible solo en la sede Tacna.”

## The consumer supplies

Only the attributes the product record has, written as supplied (`26x10cm`, `vidrio/bambú`, `1000ml`); category-specific attributes may add rows with their own visible or hidden term. Availability text comes from CE Systems per store and date; omit the line when it is not supplied.

## Do and don't

- Do keep units attached to numbers and the record's own spelling.
- Don't show an availability claim that the data does not supply, or present a past campaign's availability as current.
- Don't use the asterisk footnote style of the campaign images on web unless a footnote exists.
