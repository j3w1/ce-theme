# Callout

White floating card with a product photo, NAME, Description, specifications and price, placed over a campaign photo or a coloured panel.

## Classes

- `ce-callout`: two columns (35% image, rest text), `ce-space-20` padding, `ce-radius-callout` (20px) corners, canvas background.
- `ce-callout--bubble`: adds the curved speech-bubble tail at the top right that points at the product.
- Inside: optional `ce-code`, `ce-product-heading` (`ce-product-name` + `ce-product-description`), `ce-product-specs` or `Specs`, and the `Price` recipe at 48px.

## The consumer supplies

A product image, NAME, Description, specifications and a supplied price. Place it over photography or a brand-yellow panel where the main product is already visible in context.

## Do and don't

- Do keep the callout inside the 8% safety margin of the composition.
- Don't cover the product the campaign photo is showing.
