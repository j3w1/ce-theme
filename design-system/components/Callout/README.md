# Callout

White floating card with a product photo, identification and price, placed over a campaign photo or a colored panel.

## Classes

- `ce-callout`: two columns (35% image, rest text), `ce-space-20` padding, `ce-radius-callout` (20px) corners, canvas background.
- Inside: optional `ce-code`, an `h3`, `ce-product-details` and the `Price` recipe at 48px.

## The consumer supplies

A product image, title, short details and a supplied price. Place it over photography or a brand-yellow panel where the main product is already visible in context.

## Do and don't

- Do keep the callout inside the 8% safety margin of the composition.
- Don't cover the product the campaign photo is showing.
