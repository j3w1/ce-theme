# Asset provenance and rights

`public/assets/manifest.json` records original sources and SHA-256 hashes. The two logo files and three square campaign references were supplied by the owner for this work. Their bytes are preserved. Catalogue product images were retrieved from the company's public catalogue; their original URLs and page are recorded in the manifest.

Logo usage keeps the supplied proportions and colors. The interface uses the selected red-background palette; the transparent wordmark retains its original warmer red and yellow. A minimum 96px width and clear space equal to the height of “Casa” are initial usage guidelines, not evidence of an unseen vector master.

Only the supplied company assets and public catalogue product imagery are included. Social-story screenshots, viewers, platform controls, and IKEA reference screenshots remain outside the distributable assets. Supplied campaign images are identified as historical composition references; embedded prices are not live offers.

Casa Elida branding and photography retain their ownership and are not covered by an inferred open-source license. The repository's public visibility is not a license grant. Inter 3.015 is self-hosted from the owner-supplied files in `fonts/inter/`, recorded with SHA-256 hashes in `fonts/manifest.json`, and retains its SIL Open Font License in `fonts/inter/OFL.txt` (also served as `public/licenses/inter-OFL.txt`). The supplied archive did not include a license file; the OFL text was added from the license named in the font metadata. The outlined UI icons are original code-native SVG paths in this repository.
