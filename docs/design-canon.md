# Design canon of the CE Systems design workbench

This is the canon of the CE Systems design workbench: the rules (R-01 to R-21) that keep its simulated demonstration boards consistent. The owner approved it on 5 November 2026 for design prototypes only. It creates no business rule and approves no change to CE Theme. It lives with its boards in the published CE Systems artifacts; this copy is kept here for reference. See [the workbench guide](workbench.md) and [the change requests](change-requests/README.md). The text below is the canon as approved; board names and file names in it refer to the workbench, not to this repository.

---

# CANON — design decisions (approved by the owner, 5 November 2026) and canonical demonstration data (completion pass)

Status of every item below: **Approved by the owner (blanket approval in chat, 5 November 2026).** On that date
the owner wrote "i approve all, you complete all the remaining still opened steps". It is recorded as a blanket
approval that did not review each row individually. It covers the 21 recommended resolutions R-01 … R-21 and the
18 recommended defaults (`Decisions01Open`), taking the recommended option in each case; the tagline
«El buen gusto, no cuesta más.» staying on the utility bar; the favourites persistence default (the design's
recommended wording); the candidate photographs as illustrative media for the design prototypes; and the
demonstration NAMEs of the 16 jars (invented placeholders in `ig-jar-names.json`).

These are design decisions that make the boards consistent. They create no business rule: store prices, stock,
checkout, payment, tax and legal rules, identity method, order transitions, returns, help content, roles and
publication policy stay demonstration defaults (handoff §15 rows 3 to 11). They create no rights record:
photograph rights stay «not recorded» (no rights document was supplied). They approve no change to the shared
CE Theme. Where a board shows one of them, its annotation may keep the wording «recommended default» until the
board's next edit, or say «owner-approved design default»; it must never present one as a business rule.

## C. Rules for editing existing boards

- You may edit ONLY the boards assigned to you. Keep their layout and identity; change the minimum needed. Never touch `nav.ce-module-nav`, `canvas.json`, `ds/`, `Indice`, `Fixtures`, `Decisions*`, `Shared*`, `Flow*` unless assigned.
- Before editing a board, copy it to `$ROOT/agent-out/<key>/before/` (the integrator diffs against it).
- After editing, re-measure natural height and update `$preview` height; keep the board's width.
- Admin boards must follow R-03/R-04/R-01/R-02 wherever they state availability, scene products, revisions or pricing versions.
- Link new boards by their exact names (section D) even if the file is not written yet: the integrator will check every link at the end.
